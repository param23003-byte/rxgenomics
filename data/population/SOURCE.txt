STEP 4 - WHAT THE GENETIC ANALYSIS FOUND
================================================================================

THE HEADLINE

  10,794 results, covering 514 donors across 21 pharmacogenes.

HOW WE KNOW THE ANALYSIS IS CORRECT

  The strongest evidence is that the frequencies we observed match frequencies
  already published for South Asian populations. If the analysis were wrong, the
  numbers would not line up.

      CYP2C19 poor metabolizers    we found 16.3%   published: about 13-16%
      CYP3A5 poor/intermediate     we found 88%     the expected majority state
      NUDT15 vs TPMT               13.6% vs 3.3%    the known Asian pattern
      G6PD deficient donors        all male         G6PD is X-linked, as expected

  The G6PD finding is worth noting: the variants detected were
  'Kalyan-Kerala/Jamnagar/Rohini', the predominant G6PD deficiency variant in
  India, and 'Gond', named for the central Indian tribal population. These are
  Indian variants, found in Indian donors.
